# Scene specifications

| Scene | Composition and handoff | Mobile treatment and acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 · The Unveiling | Shaded champagne fabric gathers around an engraved sun and tide lines, revealing the framed save-the-date portrait. Corinthia calligraphy displays both given names. The sun, tide lines, arch and portrait move at different scroll rates. | Portrait framing, reversible native scroll, legible names and date, tappable cue, static reduced-motion and no-JS states. |
| 02 · A Moment in Time | Approved monochrome portrait transitions into textured paper. | Portrait crop keeps faces clear, with a gentle push and static fallback. |
| 03 · Written in the Stars | Calligraphy and pale florals on paper lead into a ring. | Legible names and florals contained around the composition. |
| 04 · Forever Begins | A wedding ring and ceremony date lead into a warm glint. | Visible date and time without relying on 3D. |
| 05 · Where We Say I Do | Verified venue photography and coastline lift into invitation paper. | Venue name and directions accessible via a verified map pin. |
| 06 · With Our Families | Printed stationery presents parents and sponsors, then opens into florals. | Structured, readable names in canonical order. |
| 07 · Standing Beside Us | Editorial floral framing and entourage names give way to fabric. | Readable role groupings, with no time limit on reading. |
| 08 · The Wedding Palette | Fabric swatches fold toward wedding notes. | Attire guidance remains readable alongside labeled colors. |
| 09 · A Few Loving Notes | Guest notes fold into an RSVP envelope. | Guest policies, gifts and after-party are easy to scan. |
| 10 · An Invitation to Respond | Envelope and response information dissolve into sunset. | Deadline and response action reachable with an approved collection method. |
| 11 · Until We Meet | Seaside sunset closes the invitation. | Portrait-safe framing with a readable date and closing message. |

Scene 01 uses procedurally drawn fabric panels, staged lighting, native sticky positioning and a scoped GSAP ScrollTrigger. The final frame uses the supplied monochrome save-the-date photograph. Scenes 02–11 are planned and require individual approval before implementation.
